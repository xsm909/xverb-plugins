# Android phones: changes

## 0.1.1 — 2026-09-29

- A storage is named in the app's language — Internal storage, SD card — not in the phone's. A folder kept under the phone's name still opens.
- The phone's camera, pictures, downloads, documents, music and movies are a submenu under it in Drives and connections, on Xverb 1.1.1.516 and later.

## 0.1.0 — 2026-09-29

- An Android phone on a USB cable as a file system: browse, read, copy both ways, make folders, rename, move and delete, over MTP.
- The phone appears in Drives and connections by itself, one row per storage, with the space free.
- Part of a file is read without the rest, so a viewer opens a large video at once.
- A folder of a thousand photographs is described in one pass the first time and remembered after.
- A copy from one place on the phone to another is done by the phone.
- macOS on Apple silicon and Intel, with libusb in the plugin.
